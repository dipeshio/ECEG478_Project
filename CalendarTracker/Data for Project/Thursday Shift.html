  To subscribe to your Conportal calendar:
  1. Copy the following link: http://www.linux.bucknell.edu/~conportal/ics/conportal-circ-366.ics
  2. Paste the calendar link into your desired calendar program.
    • For Google Calendar users:
      1. Open Google Calendar.
      2. On the left, click on the drop-down menu for "Other calendars".
      3. Click on "Add by URL".
      4. Paste the calendar link, and click "Add Calendar".
      5. There should now be a new calendar called "Techdesk".
    • For Mac Calendar users:
      1. Open Calendar.
      2. Click on File > New Calendar Subscription...".
      3. Paste the calendar link, and click "Subscribe".
      4. Modify the desired settings, and click "OK".
